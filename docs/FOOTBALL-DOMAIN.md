## 1. Why football (meaning)

Football is not “empty entertainment.” It is a **global ritual of striving, connection, and selection pressure**:

- **Connection** — shared identity across language and class; stadium + screen as simultaneous community.
- **Excellence** — continuous competition selects for skill, resilience, teamwork, tactical intelligence.
- **Evolutionary framing** — sports amplify cooperation under pressure and cultural transmission of excellence (document, don’t over-claim “science theater”).
- **Quantifiable surface** — viewership, GDP/jobs, data volume, engagement, participation.
