# Starlight Genius Forge — Competitions & Challenges

High-signal, themed competitions designed to surface and accelerate genius talent while producing shippable projects and deal flow for HNW backers.

## Competition Format (Multi-Stage Sovereign Pipeline)

1. **Announcement & Onboarding** (Community Sentinels + content on frankx.ai/arcanea.ai)
   - Theme announcement with clear rubric, prizes, timeline.
   - Talent self-nomination or agent-discovered invites.

2. **Submission Stage** (Discovery Guardians + Builder Delegates)
   - Project proposal + artifacts (code, designs, demos, lore).
   - Integrated with GenCreator Canvas for planning, Arcanea for creative elements.

3. **AI + Community Judging** (Competition Judges)
   - Structured rubric scoring (see TALENT-EVAL-RUBRIC.md).
   - Multi-model consensus + community vote via agent-orchestrated polls.
   - Feedback loops for all participants (value-first, even non-winners).

4. **Finals / Live Demos** (Builder Delegates + Investor Matchmakers)
   - Pitch events (virtual or hybrid in target regions).
   - Real-time agent-assisted Q&A and demo support.

5. **Matching & Funding Round** (Investor Matchmakers + HNW Guild)
   - Curated intros to Lumina Investors Guild.
   - Syndication, mentorship matching, micro-grant or investment opportunities.
   - Success tracking in SIS memory.

6. **Post-Competition** (Community + Marketplace)
   - Winners featured on ecosystem sites.
   - Projects listed in GenCreator Exchange / Arcanea marketplace.
   - Ongoing cohort support and follow-on competitions.

## Example Pilot Competitions (JP/PH/ID Focus)

- **Sovereign AI for Southeast Asia Impact** (PH/ID priority): Build agentic systems solving local problems (agri, education, climate, finance inclusion).
- **Japan Creative Tech Forge**: Advanced creative AI, worldbuilding, interactive experiences, robotics-adjacent.
- **Global Genius Starter Kit**: Open track for any tech/creative project demonstrating exceptional potential despite constraints.

## Prizes & Incentives (Compounding Value)

- **Talent**: Exposure, mentorship, direct HNW connections, marketplace placement, micro-funding, skill credits.
- **HNW Backers**: Exclusive deal flow, co-build rights, branding in competitions, success economics.
- **Ecosystem**: Content assets, new skills/swarm configs, reputation as the premier talent intelligence platform.
